README

Assignments 1 & 2:
My.T is the leading provider of fixed, mobile, 5G fibre internet and TV series and has the 
fastest network in Mauritius. 
Purpose: The My.T SIM Cards Registration and Follow Up System is designed to offer a 
centralised and user friendly platform for registering new SIM cards and managing follow 
ups. It simplifies data collection, applies validation rules, and produces reports, helping to 
minimise manual errors and eliminate redundant data.
